We’d like to tell you a bit more about the process we use to create personalized stories for kids so that you have a better idea of the hard work we put into “Little Stories”.

1) We search for an author. Regardless of the writer’s nationality or language, we look for unique and captivating short stories. Our main condition is that each bedtime story contains at most 50 scenes and is educational. Author’s bedtime story must be interesting for both – parents and kindergarteners. And the story shouldn’t contain more than 800 words, because it’s important to read short stories before bedtime.

2) Then we create the storyboard. It consists of rough sketches that serve as the template for the finalized drawings in the storybook, helping us decide how it will look.

3) Once the storyboard has been approved by our team, we search for an illustrator. We take great pride in matching each artist with a story that suits their style. Personal touches make our bedtime stories for kids come to life!

4) At the same time, our composer begins creating the soundtrack for the story. Inspired by the theme of the tale, he designs a gentle melody that will transport your child to relaxation and sleep (maybe you too).

5) At the same time we are looking for a professional narrator. We want to be sure our app could read aloud books for kindergarten kids. Currently you can listen to our audio books in three languages: English, Spanish and Russian. But don’t forget that we support 14 languages total.

6) After the story is finished, we translate it so that the children of the world can enjoy it in their language.

7) The story is then published in our library of books for kids.

The entire process might take anywhere from two to nine months. We take great care to provide an amazing experience. We also provide 4 free books that you can read to your baby anytime.

We are always looking for the highest quality content because we want to create the best bedtime stories for reading.
