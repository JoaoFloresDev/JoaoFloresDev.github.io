** The world's most downloaded app for toddlers to learn English **

Learn English for Kids is the ideal app for your child or baby to learn English in an easy and interactive way. 

Early Education experts agree that speaking two languages has profound beneficial effects on the brain and development of intelligence in kids. And this especially is what the app enables.

Learn English for Kids is intended for children and babies, without the need of adult guidance.

How do we achieve this? Simply playing, your child can learn the basic words of the English language, such as names of animals, colors, body parts, numbers, vehicles and many other words. 

Free of Ads this app is excellent for your child to get to know the most-spoken language in the world: English.
