Split Mobile - The Modern Way to Split Anything

Tired of chasing friends for payments or figuring out who owes what?
Split Mobile is the easiest way to split bills, track shared expenses, and collect payments instantly. Whether you’re splitting rent, dinner, drinks, groceries, concert tickets, or a weekend getaway, Split Mobile turns any group payment into a clear, automated split.

Use fractional splits, itemized totals, or quick even splits. Guests can pay with Google Pay, card, or Apple Pay, even if they don’t have the app. With built-in receipt scanning, transparent totals, and automatic reminders, you’ll never deal with awkward “I’ll get you next time” moments again.

Split Mobile is the smarter way to manage group payments, shared tabs, and real-life costs fast, accurate, and stress-free.
